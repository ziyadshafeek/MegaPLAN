STUDYBRIDGE · CHROME SIDE PANEL

YouTube to study material, without the fiddly steps.

Open a video, click the extension, choose NotebookLM or AI Studio, copy a ready prompt, and keep going. Long PDFs can be divided into numbered sections with matching prompts.

Extension guide

YouTube workflow

  1. Open a YouTube video.
  2. Open StudyBridge from Chrome's side panel.
  3. NotebookLM: copy the current YouTube URL, open NotebookLM, Add source → YouTube, paste it.
  4. AI Studio: open AI Studio, upload the video file yourself, then paste the copied prompt.
  5. Use Get transcript when the YouTube transcript panel is available.

PDF → 20 parts

  1. Select the 200-page PDF.
  2. Set Parts = 20.
  3. StudyBridge asks the FreeToolForge backend to create numbered section PDFs.
  4. Each section receives its own “Part X of 20” prompt.
  5. Create/download the PPTX sections, merge them, then compress the final deck.

Prompt: complete lecture notes

Convert this source into detailed lecture notes. Cover the source from beginning to end, preserve every important point, and do not omit examinable details.

Prompt: slide deck from one section

This is Part X of 20 of the source document. Create a dense, attractive study slide deck from this part only. Cover the source completely in order. Do not omit important points, definitions, examples, lists, numbers, tables, warnings, mechanisms or treatment details. Use concise bullets but keep the full examinable content. Add logical headings and useful mnemonics. Do not invent facts.
